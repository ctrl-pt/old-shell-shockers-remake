May 2019, preserved

The classic interface, default weapons, old sounds and lighting are restored from original files. M2DZ and SMEGG are included, alongside fourteen maps.

Choose Create to play Alpha1, Alpha2, Alpha3 or Early Bedrock: four removed layouts recovered from 2017–2018 clients. Original tile positions and rotations are preserved using the May renderer's matching models. Their spawn points and Capture the Spatula locations are reconstructed because the old map files contain no markers. The other ten maps come from the recovered May client.

This is a 0.20.1-era reconstruction. The closest complete recovered client was captured on May 19, 2019; the May 13 archived page still identifies itself as 0.18.3. The recovered client includes some nearby-patch differences, including CSG-1 damage of 105 and 15/60 ammunition.

How to play

Click PLAY, then use WASD to move, Space to jump, mouse to fire, Shift to aim, R to reload, E to switch weapons, Q for grenades, and Escape for the menu. Mouse capture works best in a normal desktop browser. In embedded browsers, move the pointer over the game to look.

Practice and multiplayer

The hosted version runs a practice room in your browser, with clearly labeled bots. Shared multiplayer is available through the included local Node server. Online multiplayer needs that server deployed on a host with WebSocket support.

Enter the address supplied by the person hosting your game. Only your browser's server selection is saved.

What was reconstructed?

The original movement, collision, weapon configuration, animations, map construction and lighting run from the archived code. The server connection, damage application, blast damage, room handling, pickup spawning and bots were reconstructed. Exact server-side damage rounding, blast falloff, lag compensation and spawning are not verified against the lost 2019 backend.

Original online accounts and purchases are unavailable. Settings and loadout are saved locally. Real purchases, advertisements, analytics and push notifications are disabled.
